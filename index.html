1. The Circle (Complete Solutions)

Formulas: Origin: $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$  |  Translated: $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$
Example 1: Standard Equation at Origin

Problem: Find the standard equation of a circle with center at $(0,0)$ and radius $r = 5$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Use origin formula: $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$
2. Substitute $r = 5$: $x^2 + y^2 = 5^2$
Final Answer: $x^2 + y^2 = 25$

Example 2: Translated Center Equation

Problem: Find the equation of a circle with center at $(2, -3)$ and radius $r = 4$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Use translated formula: $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$
2. Substitute $h = 2, k = -3, r = 4$: $(x - 2)^2 + (y - (-3))^2 = 4^2$
Final Answer: $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 16$

Example 3: Extracting Radius from Equation

Problem: Find the radius of the circle given by $x^2 + y^2 = 49$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Compare with $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$
2. Thus, $r^2 = 49 \implies r = \sqrt{49} = 7$
Final Answer: Radius $r = 7$

Example 4: Circle with Radical Radius

Problem: Write the equation of a circle with center $(-1, 5)$ and radius $r = \sqrt{3}$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Substitute $h = -1, k = 5, r = \sqrt{3}$ into $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$
2. $(x - (-1))^2 + (y - 5)^2 = (\sqrt{3})^2$
Final Answer: $(x + 1)^2 + (y - 5)^2 = 3$

Example 5: Identifying Center Coordinates

Problem: Find the center $(h, k)$ of the circle $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 81$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Match $(x - 3)$ with $(x - h) \implies h = 3$
2. Match $(y + 2)$ with $(y - k) \implies y - (-2) \implies k = -2$
Final Answer: Center is $(3, -2)$

2. The Parabola (Complete Solutions)

Formulas: Origin: $y^2 = 4px$  |  Translated: $(y - k)^2 = 4p(x - h)$
Example 1: Finding Focal Parameter $p$

Problem: Find the focal length parameter $p$ for the parabola $y^2 = 12x$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Compare $y^2 = 12x$ with standard form $y^2 = 4px$
2. Set $4p = 12 \implies p = \frac{12}{4} = 3$
Final Answer: $p = 3$

Example 2: Writing Translated Parabola Equation

Problem: Find the equation of a horizontal parabola with vertex $(-2, 1)$ and $p = 2$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Use $(y - k)^2 = 4p(x - h)$. Here $h = -2, k = 1, p = 2$.
2. $(y - 1)^2 = 4(2)(x - (-2)) \implies (y - 1)^2 = 8(x + 2)$
Final Answer: $(y - 1)^2 = 8(x + 2)$

Example 3: Extracting Vertex Coordinates

Problem: Find the vertex of the parabola $(x - 4)^2 = 16(y + 1)$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Vertical form: $(x - h)^2 = 4p(y - k)$
2. Extract $h = 4$ and $y - (-1) \implies k = -1$
Final Answer: Vertex is $(4, -1)$

Example 4: Finding Directrix Equation

Problem: Find the directrix equation for the parabola $y^2 = 8x$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Find $p$: $4p = 8 \implies p = 2$
2. Horizontal parabola directrix formula is $x = -p$
Final Answer: Directrix is $x = -2$

Example 5: Finding Focus Coordinates

Problem: Find the focus coordinates for the vertical parabola $x^2 = 16y$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Find $p$: $4p = 16 \implies p = 4$
2. Vertical parabola focus at origin is $(0, p)$
Final Answer: Focus is $(0, 4)$

3. The Ellipse (Complete Solutions)

Formulas: Origin: $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$  |  Translated: $\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1$
Example 1: Standard Ellipse Equation at Origin

Problem: Write the equation of an ellipse centered at $(0,0)$ with semi-major axis $a = 5$ and semi-minor axis $b = 3$ along the x-axis.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Use $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$
2. Substitute $a^2 = 5^2 = 25$ and $b^2 = 3^2 = 9$
Final Answer: $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$

Example 2: Translated Ellipse Equation

Problem: Find the ellipse equation with center $(-4, 3)$, $a = 5$, and $b = 3$ horizontally.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Use $\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1$
2. Substitute $h = -4, k = 3, a = 5, b = 3$: $\frac{(x+4)^2}{25} + \frac{(y-3)^2}{9} = 1$
Final Answer: $\frac{(x+4)^2}{25} + \frac{(y-3)^2}{9} = 1$

Example 3: Calculating Major Axis Length

Problem: What is the length of the major axis of the ellipse in Example 2?

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. The major axis length is always $2a$
2. Given $a = 5$, calculate $2(5) = 10$
Final Answer: Major axis length = $10$

Example 4: Identifying Translated Ellipse Center

Problem: Find the center of $\frac{(x-1)^2}{16} + \frac{(y+2)^2}{4} = 1$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Match $(x-1)$ to $(x-h) \implies h = 1$
2. Match $(y+2)$ to $(y-k) \implies y - (-2) \implies k = -2$
Final Answer: Center is $(1, -2)$

Example 5: Calculating Eccentricity ($e$)

Problem: Find the eccentricity $e$ of an ellipse where focal distance $c = 4$ and semi-major axis $a = 5$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Eccentricity formula is $e = \frac{c}{a}$
2. Substitute $c = 4$ and $a = 5 \implies e = \frac{4}{5} = 0.8$
Final Answer: Eccentricity $e = 0.8$

4. The Hyperbola (Complete Solutions)

Formulas: Origin: $\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$  |  Translated: $\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1$
Example 1: Standard Hyperbola Equation at Origin

Problem: Write the equation of a horizontal hyperbola centered at $(0,0)$ with $a = 3$ and $b = 4$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Use $\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$
2. Substitute $a^2 = 3^2 = 9$ and $b^2 = 4^2 = 16$
Final Answer: $\frac{x^2}{9} - \frac{y^2}{16} = 1$

Example 2: Translated Hyperbola Equation

Problem: Find the hyperbola equation with center $(1, -2)$, $a = 3$, and $b = 4$ horizontally.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Use $\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1$
2. Substitute $h = 1, k = -2, a = 3, b = 4$: $\frac{(x-1)^2}{9} - \frac{(y+2)^2}{16} = 1$
Final Answer: $\frac{(x-1)^2}{9} - \frac{(y+2)^2}{16} = 1$

Example 3: Calculating Transverse Axis Length

Problem: What is the transverse axis length of the hyperbola in Example 2?

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Transverse axis length is always $2a$
2. Given $a = 3$, calculate $2(3) = 6$
Final Answer: Transverse axis length = $6$

Example 4: Finding Asymptotes Equation

Problem: Find the asymptotes of the hyperbola $\frac{x^2}{9} - \frac{y^2}{16} = 1$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Horizontal hyperbola asymptotes formula is $y = \pm \frac{b}{a}x$
2. Here $a = 3, b = 4 \implies y = \pm \frac{4}{3}x$
Final Answer: Asymptotes are $y = \pm \frac{4}{3}x$

Example 5: Identifying Translated Hyperbola Center

Problem: Find the center of $\frac{(x+2)^2}{25} - \frac{(y-3)^2}{9} = 1$.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Match $(x+2) \implies x - (-2) \implies h = -2$
2. Match $(y-3) \implies k = 3$
Final Answer: Center is $(-2, 3)$
