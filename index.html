1. The Circle

Locus of points equidistant from a fixed center.

Origin (0,0): x² + y² = r²
Translated (h,k): (x - h)² + (y - k)² = r²
Review Example

Problem: Find the standard equation of a circle with center at (3, -2) and radius r = 4.

Solution: (x - 3)² + (y - (-2))² = 4²
Answer: (x - 3)² + (y + 2)² = 16

2. The Parabola

Equidistant from focus point and directrix line.

Origin (0,0): y² = 4px or x² = 4py
Translated (h,k): (y - k)² = 4p(x - h)
Review Example

Problem: Find the vertex of the parabola: (y - 1)² = 12(x + 2).

Solution: Match with (y - k)² = 4p(x - h).
Answer: Vertex is at (-2, 1)

3. The Ellipse

Sum of distances from two foci to any point is constant.

Origin (0,0): (x² / a²) + (y² / b²) = 1
Translated (h,k): ((x - h)² / a²) + ((y - k)² / b²) = 1
Review Example

Problem: Determine the center and major axis of: ((x + 4)² / 25) + ((y - 3)² / 9) = 1.

Solution: Center is (-4, 3), a² = 25 -> a = 5.
Answer: Center = (-4, 3), Major Axis = 10

4. The Hyperbola

Absolute difference of distances from two foci is constant.

Origin (0,0): (x² / a²) - (y² / b²) = 1
Translated (h,k): ((x - h)² / a²) - ((y - k)² / b²) = 1
Review Example

Problem: Identify the center of the hyperbola: ((x - 1)² / 9) - ((y + 2)² / 16) = 1.

Solution: Extract h = 1 and k = -2.
Answer: Center is at (1, -2)
